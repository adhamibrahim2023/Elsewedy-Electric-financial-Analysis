Investment Analysis

Investment Decision

Based on the financial performance analysis comparing 2015 and 2025, Elsewedy Electric has demonstrated substantial growth in scale and profitability metrics, yet increased financial risk exposure through higher leverage and tighter liquidity.

✦ Strengths

  • ROE improved significantly from 20.7% to 26.6%, delivering stronger shareholder returns
  • Net profit margin slightly improved from 6.5% to 6.8% despite scale expansion
  • Revenue grew over 12× from 20.6B to 281B EGP — massive operational scale-up
  • Both liquidity ratios remain operationally functional (Current Ratio above 1.0)
  • Long-term capital structure still predominantly equity-funded (80.3%)

✦ Risks & Concerns

  • Acid-test ratio fell below 1.0 (0.91) — reliance on inventory liquidation for short-term obligations
  • Debt-to-equity ratio surged from 2.18 to 3.32 — significantly higher financial leverage
  • Interest coverage weakened from 7.3× to 4.3× — reduced operating income cushion
  • Gross profit margin declined from 16.1% to 14.5% — cost pressure on core operations
  • 76.8% of total assets now financed by debt — elevated creditor risk
⚖
Investment Recommendation

Current shareholders are advised to hold their positions and monitor financial management closely. New investors should exercise caution and await a review of the company's debt financing policy before entering, given the heavy reliance on borrowed funds relative to equity.
